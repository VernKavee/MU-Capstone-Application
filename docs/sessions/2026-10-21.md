# Session log, 2026-10-21

Vern ran two squat sets through the app over Cloudflare quick tunnels on 2026-10-20 and
reported "Push knees out" (`knee_valgus`) firing on good reps: 2 of 12 and 9 of 19. The cause
is MediaPipe's guessed depth (z), not the knees. Fixed in SeniorProject first and ported:
the 3D shin angle stays, the direction that picks the cue is read without z and must pass
0.028 m.

## The cause

The rule averages two numbers over the frames near the bottom of the rep (knee 92 to 108):
the 3D angle between the two knee-to-ankle lines (bars 11 in, 8 out) and a direction along the
hip line, positive for knees in, of which only the sign was used. Split from the two sets'
keypoint files (the totals matched the stored `rep_stats` exactly):

| | Vern, 31 reps |
|---|---|
| 3D shin angle | 6.4 to 14.2 |
| Shin angle without z | 0.5 to 3.4 |
| Direction, x part | -0.018 to +0.015 |
| Direction, z part | +0.028 to +0.056 |
| Knees in the picture | outside the ankles in every rep |

So both the angle and the "knees in" came from z. LabMoCap (Vicon truth, the same squats
filmed from the front and at 45 degrees) confirmed it: at 45 degrees the old direction turned
Vern's real knees-out (Vicon -0.048 to -0.085) into knees-in on 3 of 4 trials; from the front
every measure matched Vicon.

## What was tried

| Idea | Result | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Angle and direction both without z, bars 5.8 and 0.028 | exam and Vern fine, but the mirrored 45 degree good set (`G_good_mirror`) got 3/5 false "Keep knees in line": there the error moves into x | no |
| Divide by cos^2 of the body yaw | right for boom (89-99% of Vicon), 24-140% for Vern | no |
| Narrow the squat placement band, or a front camera | would change the setup Vern explains to testers; the front camera likely breaks depth and back rounding | no |
| Keep the 3D angle, direction x only past 0.028 (option D) | fixes Vern, exam unchanged, both 45 degree sides fine | yes |

The 3D angle and the x-only direction read wrong in different places (z on the usual side, x on
the mirrored side), so a warning now needs both.

## How to revert

1. SeniorProject: `git revert 2a91e0b eddf6f1`.
2. Re-run `scratch.export_web_logic` into a new `rule_logic_<revert sha>` migration.
3. Here: `git revert 64c36cf`, set `ENGINE_VERSION` to the revert sha, regenerate the fixtures,
   run parity, `npx supabase migration up`.

Sets saved under each rule are told apart by `sets.engine_version`; every set keeps its keypoint
file, so either engine can replay it.
