# Session log, 2026-10-21

Vern ran two squat sets through the app over Cloudflare quick tunnels on 2026-10-20 and
reported "Push knees out" (`knee_valgus`) firing on good reps: 2 of 12 and 9 of 19. The cause
is MediaPipe's guessed depth (z), not the knees. Fixed in SeniorProject first and ported:
the 3D shin angle stays, the direction that picks the cue is read without z and must pass

## The cause

The rule averages two numbers over the frames near the bottom of the rep (knee 92 to 108):
the 3D angle between the two knee-to-ankle lines (bars 11 in, 8 out) and a direction along the
hip line, positive for knees in, of which only the sign was used. Split from the two sets'
keypoint files (the totals matched the stored `rep_stats` exactly):

| | Vern, 31 reps |
|---|---|
| 3D shin angle | 6.4 to 14.2 |
| Shin angle without z | 0.5 to 3.4 |
| Direction, x part | -0.018 to +0.015 |
| Direction, z part | +0.028 to +0.056 |
| Knees in the picture | outside the ankles in every rep |

So both the angle and the "knees in" came from z. LabMoCap (Vicon truth, the same squats
filmed from the front and at 45 degrees) confirmed it: at 45 degrees the old direction turned
Vern's real knees-out (Vicon -0.048 to -0.085) into knees-in on 3 of 4 trials; from the front
every measure matched Vicon.

## The live check at 0.020, and why the mirrored side misses knees in

Vern ran a squat workout on `50e4692` over the quick tunnels (workout `7d9c0959`) and labelled
it:
- set 1: all good, all right;
- set 2: knees in and knees out, all right except one knees-out (rep 13, angle 7.7 against the 8
  bar);
- set 3: good and knees in, alternating. Only 2 of 6 knees-in were caught.

Analysis only, no engine change. Full write-up in SeniorProject `RULES_AND_FSM.md` 5.16 (`d254681`),
script `scratch/eval/eval_knee_side_bias.py` (`d211a5a`, `3cd4cd4`).

- **It is the side, not the yaw's size.** Set 3 was filmed with the body turned the other way (yaw
  +37 against -37). The direction read every knees-in rep; the 3D angle read 4 under 11.
- **The knees-in were not smaller.** Good to knees in raised the frontal part by +12.1 on the
  mirrored side and +9.4 on the usual side. But the same good form starts at frontal +4.5 on the
  usual side and -1.3 on the mirrored side, and the bar is fixed. The September sitting agrees
  (+5.5 against -4.2).
- **LabMoCap with Vicon, two people, 45 degrees (mirrored side only):** MediaPipe pulls the frontal
  part 6 to 15 degrees toward "in", and adds a sagittal part of 10 to 15 that is not there, because the
  near shank's forward lean under-reads by 10 to 20. The front camera matches Vicon within 2.6.
- The lab has no usual-side camera at 45, so that side's error against truth is unmeasured, and
  the two mirrored knees-out reps (first workout) were both missed, which the simple "far-shank
  bias" idea does not explain.

Options for Vern, none built: a per-person, per-side starting point instead of a fixed 11; one camera
side only; the front camera for the knee rules; the signed frontal part instead of the 3D angle.

### Without the depth axis

Vern asked whether dropping z avoids the problem. Each shank's lean read from world x and y only,
signed (SeniorProject `a0a452f`, write-up in 5.16, `ad90fbe`). It keeps the side offset:
- Good form reads 4 to 6 degrees "out" on the mirrored side: -3.6 in the app, -4.7 to -8.1 in
  `G_good_mirror`, against +0.5 and -1.7 to +0.7 on the usual side. Part of each shin's forward lean
  shows up as sideways in the picture when the body is turned, and the near shin reads less of it.
- On the app's labelled reps, with bars picked on those same reps, the best no-depth bars catch
  35 or 45 of 55 faults (0 or 1 good warned), against the shipped rule's 39.
- An out bar of 5 gives `G_good_mirror` 4 of 5 false "Keep knees in line".
- Without depth it works only from the front, where the lab matches Vicon within 2.6 degrees; that
  is the front-camera option, not a separate fix.

## Push-up: the plank start and the get-up end

Vern's phone run of the landscape push-up showed the T-pose start cannot work sideways: a
landscape frame cannot hold a standing body at plank distance, and the distance that fits a
standing body is wrong for the plank anyway. Decided (Vern): the push-up starts on the plank top
and ends on getting up; facing is checked with framing before the countdown, so nobody turns in a
plank.

Evidence first (SeniorProject `4b0fc2a`, `scratch/eval/eval_plank_gate.py`), the 10 push-up
recordings at 90 degrees (6 live webcam sets, Vern's 4 phone sets):

| | result |
|---|---|
| plank hold (trusted side, all six joints 0.5, elbow and knee 150, trunk 60 or more), 8 frames | before the first rep in all 10, 0.7 to 6.4 s earlier; never standing or getting down |
| get-up end (trunk under 30, 30 frames, after 3 s) | after the last real rep in all 6 that recorded it, before the standing phantoms in 4; the phone sets end on the target |
| in-set trunk tilt | never under 66 |
| \|yaw\| in the plank | 82 +/- 12 on 94 to 100% of frames, median 85 to 89 |
| plank width, phone sets | 0.88 to 1.18 of a portrait frame, 0.49 to 0.66 scaled to landscape |

The 2026-07-16 Known Issue (MediaPipe reading a plank as 3 to 24 degrees) did not recur at 90.
