# Session log, 2026-11-05

## The UI redesign

Vern's goal from 2026-11-03: fix the UI. What bothered him on the phone: missing back buttons,
the live count's "of N" hard to read, guide text too small for a phone, and a weak palette. He
asked to see a reference and a palette before any code changed.

### Mockups, three rounds

All rounds are versions of one private artifact, https://claude.ai/artifact/1SYEcngZMnnE2BPPvDiq7W.

1. Three palettes on hand-drawn screens (near-black and lime, navy and cyan, today's charcoal
   fixed), with the live count on a solid panel. Rejected: "When I use an app, I want to see
   myself biggest"; a box in front of the body hides the fault the warning names. He asked for
   design skills instead.
2. `frontend-design` (anthropics, 1.1M installs) and `ui-ux-pro-max` (nextlevelbuilder, 435K)
   installed in the repo (`.claude/skills/`), not globally, at Vern's request. Three directions
   from the skill's data (clinical teal, fitness orange, a motion-lab mint). The live screen
   kept the body full size with an outlined count in the top corner, which Vern accepted. He
   asked for a smaller rest timer and similarity per body part, then the coach's feedback on the
   rest screen; the palettes were "too bad". The skill's colour rows are stock Tailwind shades on
   slate, which is why they read as generic.
3. Three palettes from Radix Colors scales (Iris, Daylight, Olive), every text pair checked at
   4.5:1 or better. Vern chose Daylight and asked for body parts above the feedback.

Asked separately: the typeface. Big Shoulders was his Phase 6 choice; every mockup used Barlow
Condensed and Barlow. He chose Barlow.

### 2. Daylight palette (`4dc32fa`)

`ink` used to mean both page text and text over video. It was split: `ink`, `tape`, `tape-ink`
stay the colours over video; the page got `background` #f9f9f8, `surface`, `surface-2`, `line`,
`foreground` #21201c, `muted` #63635e, `accent` #3e63dd, `ok` #2a7e3b. Every page-side `ink`
moved to the page tokens; the live stage and the replay player are `.stage`, where the focus ring
stays off-white (amber on sand is about 1.5:1, under the 3:1 a focus ring needs). Thirty muted
lines drawn with `opacity-60` to `-80` moved to `text-muted`, which holds 5.7:1. The week chart's
bars moved from tape to the accent, so amber means only a warning. `viewport` is light.
