## Frame rules wait 0.15 s, not 5 frames (Vern)

Plan A of the two engine plans (the toe line, plan B, waits for its test sitting). The four
frame rules (squat `rounded_back`, lunge `forward_trunk_lean`, curl `elbow_flare`, push-up
`hip_sag_or_pike`) fired after 5 violated frames in a row, which lasted 0.38 s at 13 fps and
0.09 s at 58. They now fire once violated for 0.15 s.

**Definition.** A frame rule fires on the first frame where `now - start >= 150 ms`, `start`
being the first violated frame of the current run. A false or null verdict resets the run, as
before. It is the only definition that gives 3 frames at 13 fps, 7 at 35 and 10 at 58, the
numbers the comparison of 2026-10-23 used.

**Gap (Vern's choice).** Frames the engine skips (low confidence) never reach the evaluator, so
with a time a stall could count as held time: a bad frame at 0 ms, nothing seen for 300 ms,
a bad frame at 333 ms would warn on two frames. A gap longer than the wait between two frames
the evaluator sees now starts the run over. A short drop of 1 or 2 frames keeps it.
