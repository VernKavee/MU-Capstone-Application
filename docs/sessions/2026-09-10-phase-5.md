## Decisions made

- The chart covers the last seven days ending today in the browser's time zone, at Vern's
  request; REQUIREMENTS section 5 says Monday to Sunday. Each day is the mean of its sets'
  overall similarity, initial and repair, sets of workouts left part way included.
- The chart's form: a fixed 0 to 100 scale with a faint line at 100; every column carries
  its value and its set count, so there is no axis and no tooltip; a day with sets but no
  score shows a dash. The panel is the live screen's dark ground, because tape yellow on
  white fails the 3:1 a bar needs; yellow on it is 11.3:1 and the muted text 4.8:1 or more.
  The columns rise once on load, and not under reduced motion.
- Time zone: the browser writes its zone into the `tz` cookie and refreshes once when it
  first arrives or changes. The server checks it names a real zone and otherwise uses its
  own. Every date the server renders passes the zone explicitly.
- Active day: a day with a finished workout of the exercise, dated by the workout's start,
  over the same seven days. A workout left part way counts on the chart and nowhere else in
  History (Vern's choice).
- Level 3 lists finished workouts only, newest first, with the start, the setup, the
  number of repair sets, and the mean over the workout's scored sets. No paging.
- Level 4 shows every set as a tile with similarity, correct reps, attempts, and a note
  when it ended short or its repair set was skipped; `?set=` chooses one, the first by
  default. Tiles show correct reps and attempts rather than a violation count, because a
  count would fetch every set's attempt records. Only the chosen set's attempts, feedback,
  and file paths are fetched.
- Replay: the browser fetches the two files from storage only when Play or an attempt is
  pressed, a signed URL for the video and a download for the keypoint file, gunzipped with
  `DecompressionStream`. The video plays as recorded, not mirrored. Joints are read through
  the file's own `fields`. The file holds no violations per frame, so an attempt's
  violations light their joints for the whole attempt, with the messages as a caption. The
  attempt strip, the playhead, and the clock come from the file's `t`, never from the
  video's duration. A set missing one of its files, or whose keypoint file holds fewer than
  two frames, is not replayed.
- The deep-dive widens the (app) column to 64rem through `has-[[data-wide]]` in the layout;
  every other page keeps the phone-width column until the Phase 6 pass.
- Big Shoulders Display now also sets the deep-dive's one big number, the set's similarity.
- The feedback detail screen is per set, like the feedback itself.
- No migration: the Phase 4 policies and grants cover every read.
