## Note for the next session: push-up "Body not straight" from the far ankle

Found on Vern's first laptop run (2026-10-04, after the port). Not fixed; a rule change is
Vern's call and goes into the Python first.

**Symptom.** Push-up sets warn "Body not straight" (`hip_sag_or_pike`, bar 160, frame
scope, 10 frames) on reps that were close to straight. Warned reps bottom out at 153.7 to
157.7; reps counted correct sit at 157 to 159 and escape only through the 10-frame debounce.

**Cause.** At the 45 degree push-up angle the far ankle is hidden behind the legs and
MediaPipe guesses it. The rule averages both sides' shoulder-hip-ankle angles, and the
guessed far side reads 8 to 10 degrees lower, pulling the mean onto the bar.

| Set | Far side | Far ankle score | Far side angle (median) | Near side angle (median) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fa8cee30` | left | 0.18 | 158.6 | 168.7 |
| `19ca5f09` | left | 0.27 | 157.9 | 166.3 |
| `ecff1c37` | left | 0.16 | 160.6 | 171.1 |
| `d309dd64` (turned the other way) | right | 0.24 | 159.3 | 167.6 |

- The low side follows the camera, not the body: when Vern turned the other way the low
  reading moved to the right side.
- The near ankle scores 0.79 to 0.95. On the near side the hip sits 1 to 2.5 cm off the
  shoulder-ankle line, slightly high: no real sag or pike.
- Warnings over the same 34 attempts (10-frame streak under 160): mean of both sides, as
  shipped, 10 (matches the saved sets exactly); near side only 4; far side only 29. Two of
  the near-side 4 are abandoned attempts with minima of 113 and 130, probably getting into
  or out of the plank; the video would confirm.
- The same far-side problem as open item 17, which the floor bar fixed for push-up depth.

**Options, for Vern to decide.**
1. Read the side whose shoulder, hip, and ankle are all trusted (for example the higher
   minimum score of the three), as the floor bar picks its arm by elbow score. Recommended.
2. Take the larger of the two sides' angles: simpler, but hides a fault only the near side
   shows.
3. Lower the bar: not recommended, it loosens the rule for real sag and pike.

Any change goes Python first (`_mean_body_alignment` / `_pushup_hip_sag_or_pike` and the
accumulator's `min_body_alignment` in `src/rule_based/form_rules.py`), then re-check the
exam push-up recordings (the scorecard has exam sag at 4 of 5 and must not lose them), then
the export, a new migration, the TypeScript port in `lib/engine/rules.ts`, and the parity
golden and fixtures regenerated, with `ENGINE_VERSION` bumped.
